# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University of Ottawa undergraduates planning their semester. They arrive stressed about course conflicts, prerequisite chains, and registration deadlines. Primary task: generate a workable timetable fast, then enrol.

## Product Purpose

uoplan replaces the clunky uoCampus scheduling flow with a fast, intelligent timetable generator. Students map their degree requirements, pick constraints, and get conflict-free schedules in seconds. Success is a student confidently enrolled in a full course load without touching uoCampus manually.

## Brand Personality

Clean, precise, calm. The interface signals competence without shouting. Students should feel like they're using a serious tool built by someone who actually attends the university.

## Anti-references

uoCampus and Brightspace (boxy, dated, institutional). Generic SaaS dashboard templates (hero metrics, gradient cards, confetti illustrations).

## Design Principles

1. Speed over ceremony: every action should feel immediate. No confirmation dialogs unless destructive.
2. Confidence through clarity: labels, states, and feedback that leave no ambiguity about what just happened.
3. Dense without busy: pack information without visual noise. Restraint in decoration, generosity in information.
4. Dark because focused: a focused student at a desk at night, reading a schedule. Dark canvas, light content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort. Reasonable contrast, keyboard-navigable flows, visible focus indicators. No formal WCAG compliance target.
