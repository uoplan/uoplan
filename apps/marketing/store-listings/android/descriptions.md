## Full description (max 4000 characters)

uoPlan is an independent course planner and timetable generator for University of Ottawa students. Pick your term, program and the courses you've already finished, and uoPlan turns your remaining degree requirements into conflict-free weekly timetables — in seconds.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the University of Ottawa. uoPlan is an independent, student-built project. The names "University of Ottawa" and "uOttawa" are used only to describe the school this tool is built for.

PLAN YOUR DEGREE, NOT JUST YOUR TERM
• Choose your program and mark the courses you've already completed.
• uoPlan reads your degree requirements and shows exactly what you still need.
• Generate conflict-free weekly timetables that satisfy those requirements automatically.
• Compare options and lock in the sections you want.

EXPLORE COURSES, PROFESSORS AND PROGRAMS
• Search the full course catalogue by code, title, discipline, level or language.
• See real grade distributions, class averages, pass rates and difficulty at a glance.
• Browse the hardest courses, highest fail rates and most-graded classes.
• Open any course for evaluation breakdowns, prerequisites and historical grades.

GRADE TRENDS BACKED BY REAL DATA
• Millions of historical graded results, visualised.
• See how a course's grades have shifted over the years.
• Make informed choices about what — and with whom — to take.

BUILT TO RESPECT YOUR PRIVACY
• No account, no login, no tracking.
• Import your transcript as a PDF — it's parsed entirely on your device and never uploaded.
• Export your generated timetable straight to your calendar.
• Optional local reminders for your classes.

STUDENT-RUN, NO ADS, NO PAYWALLS
uoPlan is built and maintained by students, for students. There are no ads and no paywalls, and every feature is available to everyone. Optional donations help cover upkeep — they're voluntary and never unlock features.

Questions or feedback? Visit uoplan.party.

uoPlan is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the University of Ottawa. All course, schedule and grade information is provided for planning purposes only — always confirm requirements and registration details with official University of Ottawa sources.
